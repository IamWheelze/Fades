Chief Executive Officer

Folashade "Shade" Egbodofo

Founder & CEO

With over 25 years of proven excellence in multi-industry trading, importation, and business management, Folashade Egbodofo is a distinguished leader in African and global markets. Her hands-on experience spans agriculture, commerce, and international trade, successfully managing multimillion-naira operations with consistent profitability and growth.

A graduate with a Bachelor's degree in Business Management, Shade combines formal education with practical expertise in farm operations, poultry management, fish farming, and livestock development. Her deep operational knowledge and strong negotiation skills have established Fades Marketing Company as a trusted name across multiple sectors.

Known for her integrity, professionalism, and unwavering commitment to results, Shade has built long-term marketing and distribution networks that span Africa and international markets. Her leadership philosophy centers on excellence, ethical business practices, and creating sustainable value for clients and partners.
